Welcome to Noonari Farms

Noonari Farms is a multi-disciplinary farming enterprise dedicated to excellence in Agriculture, Livestock, and Fisheries.

Situated along Badin Road, Hyderabad, our farm combines modern sustainable farming techniques with traditional care to produce high-grade crops, healthy livestock, and fresh aquatic harvests.

We are committed to delivering natural, fresh, and superior quality products to local markets and commercial partners across the region.

Our Core Divisions

Agriculture

Cultivating seasonal crops, fodder, and high-yield agricultural produce using sustainable and eco-friendly soil practices.

Livestock

Rearing healthy cattle and livestock managed under strict veterinary hygiene and optimal nutritional standards.

Fisheries

Modern fish farming in high-capacity ponds ensuring fresh, clean, and nutritious fish supply straight from the water.
